FAMU Legislative Internship Program

Prof. Jonathan Blackmon, Dr. Brandon Armstrong and Dr. Abdul Sharif
Applications for the 2027 FAMU Legislative Internship Program are officially open. Ten students will be selected for the 2027 cohort to work directly inside the office of an elected member of the Florida Legislature, with selection decided through a competitive interview process. Students who have an interest in gaining professional experience in public policy, governance, legislative affairs, law, and public service are encouraged to apply. Please note, this paid opportunity is only available to Political Science majors.

This is a front-row seat inside the Florida Legislature itself: observing floor sessions, sitting in on committee work, and contributing to the daily business of a sitting elected official during Florida's legislative session. Few undergraduate opportunities put you this close to how state government actually operates.

  • Currently enrolled full-time students only
  • Minimum cumulative GPA of 3.0
  • Enroll in PUP 4947-301 (Public Affairs Internship) for Spring 2027
  • Up to 20 hours of internship work each week
  • Program runs March 2 through April 30, 2027
