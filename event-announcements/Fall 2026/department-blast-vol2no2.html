Diaspora Dialogues: African Diaspora Solidarities, The Haitian and African American Experience
Thursday, October 15, 2026 12:00 PM – 1:15 PM · Meek-Eaton Black Archives

Prof. Ayinde Madzimoyo · FAMU Center for African and African American Studies. A community dialogue on the historic bonds between African Americans and Haitians, from the legacy of the Haitian Revolution to the political and social challenges both communities face today. If you are interested in comparative politics, migration, or the Caribbean, come ready to talk.

Diaspora Dialogues: Afro-Mexicanos, The Resilience of Black Culture in Yanga, Veracruz
Thursday, November 19, 2026 12:00 PM – 1:15 PM · Meek-Eaton Black Archives

Prof. Sarah Dominguez. Long before abolition across the Americas, a free Black community thrived in Yanga, Veracruz. This session covers the history, culture, and staying power of Afro-Mexicanos, a chapter of the African Diaspora most of us never got in a textbook. Put it on your calendar now.

Organization Spotlight
Pi Sigma Alpha, The National Political Science Honor Society

Pi Sigma Alpha is the national honor society for political science, and FAMU has its own chapter. Membership recognizes the work you have already put in, and it follows you: you wear the cords at graduation and the line goes on your resume for law school, grad school, and job applications.

It is more than a line on a resume. Members can present at the National Student Research Conference, submit to the Undergraduate Journal of Politics, compete for paper and thesis awards, and apply for national scholarships, including the McManus Internship Scholarship for D.C. internships and the Penniman Graduate School Scholarship.
