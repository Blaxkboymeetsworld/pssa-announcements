NSA Summer 2027 Internship Program, Colorado Summer Program
⚠ Deadline: September 5, 2026 Paid · Denver/Aurora, CO or Fort Meade, MD

National Security Agency. A 12-week paid internship open to undergraduate freshmen through PhD students, with positions in software engineering, target analysis, and installations and logistics. Preferred GPA of 3.0 or higher. Students living more than 75 miles from Denver get housing and travel support. Requires U.S. citizenship and eligibility for a TS/SCI clearance, which includes a background investigation, a polygraph, and a psychological evaluation.

Your application package needs a transcript and a current resume emailed directly to SummerInterns@uwe.nsa.gov in addition to the online application. This is one of more than 30 NSA summer internships. Browse the rest if this specific one is not your fit.

Fall 2026 Cycle, Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship
Deadline: October 1, 2026 Paid · $5,000 · Study or Intern Abroad

U.S. Department of State. I am a Gilman alum myself, mine took me to Uruguay to catalog Afro-descendant archives, and it is still one of the most accessible study or intern abroad scholarships out there because it is built specifically for students with financial need. If you are a current Pell Grant recipient (or will be one during your term abroad) and a U.S. citizen, apply.

Studying a critical need language can add $3,000 on top of your base award. This cycle covers Spring, Summer, and Fall 2027 programs, plus Academic Year 2027-28.

Research Assistant Program, Federal Reserve Board of Governors
Applications Open Now Deadline: October 15, 2026

Washington, D.C. · Two-year program. If you have coursework in economics, statistics, or quantitative methods and you are even thinking about a PhD, this is one of the strongest credentials you can build before grad school. RAs work directly with PhD economists on original research and policy analysis. The Board covers tuition assistance and relocation. U.S. citizenship required.

You need a bachelor's or master's in economics, statistics, math, finance, data science, computer science, public policy, or a related field, plus familiarity with at least one programming language: Stata, R, Python, whatever you have got.

Empirical Research Fellowship, Stanford Law School
First-Round Deadline: October 23, 2026 Paid · $65,312–$72,800/yr · Stanford, CA

Working with economist Dr. John J. Donohue and other Stanford Law faculty. Political science is named explicitly among the qualifying disciplines here, alongside economics, statistics, and computer science. Fellows spend a full year, with a possible second year, on policy relevant empirical research: building datasets, running statistical analysis, co-authoring reports. Past fellows have gone on to PhD programs at Berkeley, Columbia, Harvard, and MIT, and to law school at Stanford and Yale.

Programming experience in R, Python, or Stata is strongly preferred, but you can learn fast if you are willing. Fellows can also audit Stanford courses for free during the fellowship.

2027-28 Fellowship Cycle, Princeton in Africa
Applications Just Opened Deadline: October 26, 2026

Open to graduates of any accredited four-year U.S. or African university. A twelve-month fellowship and one of the best ways to get a real foot in the door on the continent, in governance, development, media, public health, whatever track you are on. You need to be graduating by June 2027 or already hold a bachelor's, master's, or PhD, be under 35, and have full professional proficiency in English. No application fee.

Fellows cover a $350 placement fee and travel costs; housing, a living stipend, and medical insurance are covered by your host organization and Princeton in Africa. An information session for prospective applicants runs September 16.

Florida Governor's Fellows Program
Applications Open Now Deadline: January 2027

Executive Office of the Governor · Tallahassee, FL. A full academic year working directly inside the Executive Office of the Governor and state agencies, open to full-time undergraduate juniors and seniors, graduate students, and immediate grads statewide. Fellows attend weekly leadership sessions with the Governor, Cabinet officers, and agency heads, take policy trips to Camp Blanding, Kennedy Space Center, and Washington, D.C., and close the year presenting an independent policy proposal.

Fellows are paid at least $18/hr as OPS employees, plus a $1,000 stipend if you already live in Tallahassee or $2,000 to help with relocation if you do not. Tuition is covered in most cases.

Organization Spotlight
Young Professionals in Foreign Policy (YPFP)

This one is personal. I just wrapped a term as the 2026 U.S. Energy Delegate to the Y20 Summit, the official youth engagement arm of the G20, through YPFP. Delegates from every G20 country negotiate a formal policy communique that gets handed directly to G20 Heads of State. This year's summit ran August 10 to 14 in Washington, D.C., and the finalized communique has already been delivered to world leaders.

Also worth following: Carmen Shaw, this year's Head Delegate for Food Security and Global Health and Co-Editor of The Pandora Report, GMU's biodefense policy newsletter. Her work sits at the intersection of biotechnology, AI governance, and global health security. Read her Q&A if international health security is your track.

Looking ahead: the United Kingdom holds the G20 presidency in 2027, so the next Y20 Summit will be there. YPFP recruits and manages the entire American delegation no matter where it is held, so this is a real path to represent the United States abroad, or just a strong network if energy policy, diplomacy, or international affairs is your track. Applications for the 2027 delegate cohort are not open yet. Join YPFP now so you do not miss the announcement.
